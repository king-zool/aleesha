A few things to know

Luggage, pickup and changes to your plans.

Check the allowance shown for your chosen vehicle. If you have a large trunk or extra bags, confirm space and any charge with the operator before travelling.
The pickup point should be confirmed with your operator before departure. Keep your phone available for updates.
Contact the operator as soon as your plans change. Availability and any rescheduling charge depend on the trip.
This project saves a reservation request on your device. It does not take payments or issue a confirmed travel ticket.
